Deep Learning-Based Battery Health Prediction

An independent reproduction of a published battery-health study, connecting my electrical engineering background with AI. The published project estimates lithium-ion battery State of Health (SOH) using a hybrid CNN–TCN–LSTM–attention network and real NASA PCoE, Oxford, and CALCE data.

PythonPyTorchDeep learningTime seriesBattery SOH
CNN→TCN→LSTM→Attention

A reproducible evaluation

The repository reports five independent runs of stratified five-fold cross-validation on each dataset. The pipeline includes incremental-capacity, differential-voltage, and differential-capacity features, with committed experiment summaries and figures.

Read the results ↗View the architecture ↗

DT-AttNet · MSc AI COURSEWORK

Exploring AI interaction risks.
Through temporal learning.

A Dual-Head Temporal Attention Network exploring addiction-risk classification from simulated user behavioural sequences. The research connects a critique of real-time, hyper-personalized generative AI with a computational experiment using a nonlinear “Compulsive Spiral” data generator.

PythonPyTorch1D-CNNBiLSTMSelf-attentionContrastive learning
Explore DT-AttNet ↗Read the experiment report ↗
SYNTHETIC-DATA RESEARCH

  • Sequential model architecture

    1D-CNN, bidirectional LSTM, and multi-head self-attention for behavioural sequence classification.

  • Dual-head optimization

    Cross-entropy combined with supervised contrastive learning; evaluated alongside baselines and ablations.

  • Reported full-model accuracy

    97.96% ± 1.16% across five random seeds, according to the detailed report. Baseline MLP: 98.66% ± 0.90%.

Results are repository-reported on synthetic data, not clinical validation or evidence of real-world addiction prediction. Training was not rerun here. The detailed full-model result is used instead of the README’s broader “>98%” summary.

CREDIT RISK · MACHINE LEARNING COURSEWORK

Understanding credit risk.
Through data-driven models.

Credit Risk Prediction Agent explores borrower and historical data to classify repayment or default risk. The published coursework notebook compares traditional machine learning approaches to support more informed lending decisions.

PythonpandasNumPyscikit-learnMatplotlibSeaborn
Explore the repository ↗
NOTEBOOK IMPLEMENTATION

  • Three classification approaches

    Gaussian Naïve Bayes, Random Forest, and k-nearest neighbours.

  • Model selection

    Stratified 75/25 train–test split and grid-search cross-validation for Random Forest and kNN.

  • Evaluation beyond accuracy

    Precision, recall, F1, and ROC-AUC to examine classifier performance.

The implementation was inspected inside the repository’s coursework archive. Models were not rerun here; no performance score or production lending readiness is claimed.
