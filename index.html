Battery Health Prediction

A hybrid CNN–TCN–LSTM–attention network for lithium-ion State-of-Health estimation on NASA, Oxford, and CALCE data.

Inside the project +

Five independent runs of stratified five-fold cross-validation per dataset. Features include incremental capacity, differential voltage, and differential capacity.

Oxford SOH RMSE0.0215 ± 0.0045
NASA SOH RMSE0.0417 ± 0.0023
CALCE SOH RMSE0.0544 ± 0.0147

Repository-reported results, not rerun here. Oxford aligns with the paper’s 0.021 target; NASA does not. Published scope is SOH, with RUL work unpublished.

DT-AttNet

A dual-head temporal attention network exploring AI addiction-risk classification through simulated behavioural sequences.

Inside the project +

1D-CNN, BiLSTM, multi-head self-attention, and supervised contrastive learning. Evaluated across five seeds on a nonlinear “Compulsive Spiral” synthetic dataset.

Full-model accuracy97.96% ± 1.16%
MLP baseline accuracy98.66% ± 0.90%

Detailed report results on synthetic data; training was not rerun. This is research, without clinical validation or claims of real-world addiction prediction.

Credit Risk Prediction Agent

Comparing machine learning classifiers to explore repayment and default risk using borrower and historical data.

Inside the project +

Gaussian Naïve Bayes, Random Forest, and k-nearest neighbours. Uses a stratified 75/25 split, grid-search cross-validation, and accuracy, precision, recall, F1, and ROC-AUC evaluation.

Notebook inspected inside the repository’s coursework archive. No model performance score or production lending readiness is claimed; training was not rerun.
